Can you install awnings outside Mumbai?
Yes. CanovaShades works across Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai and Pune and delivers pan-India projects.
How do I choose between fixed and retractable awnings?
Fixed systems provide permanent cover. Retractable and motorised systems are better when you want to control sunlight, rain cover and the view of the sky.
Are motorised awnings available?
Yes. Motorised and retractable awning systems are available for windows, terraces, cafes, hospitality spaces and other outdoor areas.
