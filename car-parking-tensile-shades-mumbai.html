PARKING SHADE STRUCTURES

Protect vehicles without making the parking area feel heavy.

A well-designed car parking tensile shade uses a light membrane roof, engineered steelwork and considered column placement to provide cover while keeping circulation and sightlines open.

CanovaShades handles the structure from design and load checks through steel fabrication, membrane installation and site erection. The result is a parking shade designed for the actual bays, access and weather exposure.

What goes into a car parking tensile shade?

Site-led layout

Bay count, vehicle movement, clearances and existing foundations are considered before fabrication.

Engineered steelwork

Posts, beams, cables, fittings and anchors are detailed for the span and site conditions.

Weather-ready membrane

UV-stable, flame-retardant membrane options provide shade and rain cover for the parking area.
