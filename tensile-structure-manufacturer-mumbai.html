One team for the complete tensile structure.

CanovaShades combines design, engineering and fabrication under one roof. Each structure is drawn to its site, checked for the relevant loads and made with materials selected for Indian sun, coastal air and monsoon weather.

Our tensile structure work ranges from entrance canopies and car parking shades to free-form roofs, courtyards, atriums, walkways and outdoor hospitality spaces. The team works across Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Pune and pan-India locations.

COMMON APPLICATIONS

Engineered shade for homes, businesses and hospitality.

CanovaShades builds tensile roofs and membrane structures for residential entrances, commercial campuses, cafes, hotels, resorts, courtyards, parking areas and public walkways.

How long does a tensile structure take?
Smaller residential canopies and pergolas are typically fabricated and installed in 7 to 15 days. Larger roofs depend on steel fabrication, site access and span.
Do you install outside Mumbai?
Yes. CanovaShades regularly works across Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai and Pune and delivers pan-India projects.
Which tensile fabric is used?
PVDF-lacquered PVC-coated polyester is used as standard, with PTFE and ETFE available for landmark projects. Material selection depends on the span, use and site conditions.
