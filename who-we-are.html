WHO WE ARE

A studio of designers, engineers and fabricators — building shaded spaces since 2008.

OUR STORY

A studio practice built around one obsession: honest structure.

CanovaShades was founded in Mumbai on a simple belief — that designing, engineering and fabricating a structure should never be split across three companies. Under one roof, our team takes an ambitious sketch and returns a finished, standing build.

We design and install canopies, pergolas and tensile membrane structures for homes, offices, cafes, hotels and resorts across India — from a single doorway awning to a full courtyard roof.

What holds every project together is a single standard: what we draw is what gets built, detailed with materials chosen for Indian sun, wind and monsoon.

WHAT GUIDES US

Four principles, no exceptions.

Design Integrity

Every form serves both engineering and eye.

Craft in-house

Fabricated, welded and finished by our own team.

Built to Last

Materials and detailing tuned for Indian conditions.

On-Time Delivery

Schedules honoured. That is non-negotiable.

OUR APPROACH

Design once. Detail obsessively. Build honestly.

We work in tight iteration cycles — architect, engineer, and fabricator side by side from day one. Every joint is drawn. Every detail is prototyped. Every schedule is committed.
